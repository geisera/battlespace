## Initiative

Initiative determines which player controls the tempo of the turn. It is resolved every turn during the Initiative Phase, after power allocations are revealed.

### Determining Initiative

Each player calculates an initiative total for their ship:

**2d6 + Command Rating + modifiers**

| Modifier | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Power allocated to Electronics (per point, max +3) | +1 |
| Sensors damaged or degraded | −2 |
| Currently jammed | −2 |
| Command disruption (per level) | −1 |
| Bridge or command system critical | −2 |
| Enemy target lock on your ship | −1 |

Compare totals. The higher total holds initiative for this turn.

### Tie Breakers

Resolve ties in the following order:

1. Higher Command Rating
2. Scenario priority, or previous-turn holder (choose one as a standing rule before play)

### Effects of Initiative

The initiative holder:

* Acts with flexible priority in Action Phase A, and may **defer** to force the opponent to act first without giving up their own actions.
* Acts second in Action Phase B, gaining the final action of the tactical exchange.

The non-initiative player:

* Must respond to the initiative holder's choices in Phase A.
* Acts first in Phase B, with no defers allowed by either side.

### Multi-Ship Games

With more than two players, rank all initiative totals from highest to lowest. In Phase A, priority passes down the ranking; a defer passes priority to the next-ranked player who has not passed. In Phase B, players act in reverse initiative order.
