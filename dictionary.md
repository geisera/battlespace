# Battlespace Dictionary

A concise reference for core ship-construction and combat terms.

## Armor

Directional protection assigned to a ship's facings.

**Purpose:** Reduce or absorb incoming damage before it reaches the hull.

**Effects:**
- Armor is distributed across ship facings.
- No facing may have 0 Armor.
- Damage is resolved against the Armor on the struck facing.
- Armor adds Mass and may require additional Superstructure.

---

## Base Signature

The ship's inherent detectability before temporary modifiers are applied.

```text
Base Signature = Mass Factor
```

**Purpose:** Provide the starting value for detection.

**Effects:**
- Larger ships are easier to detect.
- Active sensors, engine use, reactor emissions, stealth systems, and environmental effects may modify Effective Signature.

---

## Component Mass

The combined Mass of all installed components, excluding Superstructure.

**Purpose:** Contribute to Total Mass and Hull.

---

## Engine

A propulsion component purchased at Rating 1-6.

**Purpose:** Convert Reactor Power into Movement Points efficiently.

**Effects:**
- A ship must carry at least one Engine.
- A ship may carry multiple Engines.
- Each Engine adds Mass and requires Superstructure.
- Engine Ratings are added together to determine Total Engine Rating.

---

## Engine Rating

The Rating of an individual Engine.

**Range:** 1-6.

**Purpose:** Measure the propulsion capacity of that Engine.

---

## Effective Signature

The ship's current detectability after situational modifiers.

A typical model is:

```text
Effective Signature =
Base Signature
+ Emission Modifiers
- Stealth Modifiers
+ Environmental Modifiers
```

**Purpose:** Determine how difficult the ship is to detect or track.

---

## Hull

The ship's general damage capacity.

**Purpose:** Measure how much ordinary physical damage the ship can sustain before critical structural failure becomes likely.

**Effects:**
- Normal weapon damage generally affects Hull after protection is resolved.
- Hull is distinct from Structure.
- Hull represents compartments, machinery, plating, and other non-frame material.

---

## Mass Factor

A simplified measure of the ship's inertia and physical bulk.

```text
Mass Factor = Total Mass / 10, rounded up
```

**Purpose:** Convert Total Mass into a practical combat value.

**Effects:**
- Used to calculate Movement Power Cost.
- May be used for collisions, towing, displacement, or other mass-sensitive effects.
- Mass Factor should not serve as a universal penalty.

---

## Maximum MP

The maximum number of Movement Points a ship may generate during an Action Phase.

**Purpose:** Represent the ship's maximum practical thrust or maneuvering ceiling.

**Effects:**
- Prevents highly efficient ships from converting unlimited Reactor Power into movement.
- The exact formula is determined by the maneuver rules.

---

## Movement Point (MP)

A temporary tactical resource used to change a ship's position or orientation.

**Purpose:** Measure how much maneuvering a ship can perform during an Action Phase.

Typical expenditures may include:

```text
Advance 1 hex      = 1 MP
Turn 1 hex-side    = 1 MP
Special maneuver   = varies
```

**Effects:**
- MP is generated from Power allocated to propulsion.
- MP is normally spent during the current Action Phase.
- MP is not a fixed ship characteristic.

---

## Movement Power Cost

The amount of Reactor Power required to generate 1 Movement Point.

```text
Movement Power Cost =
Mass Factor / Total Engine Rating,
rounded up,
minimum 1
```

**Purpose:** Measure propulsion efficiency.

**Effects:**
- Low Movement Power Cost makes a ship power-efficient.
- High Movement Power Cost forces a ship to devote more Reactor Power to maneuvering.

---

## Power

The resource generated by the ship's Reactor and allocated among ship systems.

**Purpose:** Serve as the ship's primary tactical action economy.

Power may be allocated to:

- Engines
- Shields
- Weapons
- Sensors
- Special systems

---

## Propulsion Power

Reactor Power allocated to the ship's Engines.

**Purpose:** Generate Movement Points.

A typical calculation is:

```text
MP Generated =
Propulsion Power / Movement Power Cost,
rounded down
```

MP generated may not exceed Maximum MP.

---

## Reactor

The component that generates Power each round.

**Purpose:** Supply the ship's systems.

**Effects:**
- Every ship must carry one Reactor.
- Reactor Output is selected during construction.
- Larger-output Reactors have greater Mass and Superstructure Cost.

---

## Reactor Output

The total Power generated by the ship's Reactor each round.

**Purpose:** Determine the ship's total Power budget.

**Effects:**
- Power must be divided among competing systems.
- A legal ship must have enough Reactor Output to purchase at least 1 MP.

```text
Reactor Output >= Movement Power Cost
```

---

## Sensor Array

A component purchased at Rating 1-6.

**Purpose:** Detect and track other ships and objects.

**Effects:**
- Sensor Rating determines or contributes to detection range.
- Sensor Rating may cap the amount of Power allocated to sensors.

---

## Sensor Rating

The Rating of the installed Sensor Array.

**Range:** 1-6.

**Purpose:** Represent the capability and operating ceiling of the ship's sensors.

---

## Size Class

A categorical measure derived from Total Mass.

| Total Mass | Size Class |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1-15 | 1 |
| 16-35 | 2 |
| 36-70 | 3 |
| 71-120 | 4 |
| 121+ | 5 |

**Purpose:** Handle rules that depend on broad physical scale rather than precise Mass.

Possible uses include:

- Target-size interactions
- Docking restrictions
- Boarding
- Collision relationships
- Scenario limits
- Line-of-sight effects

---

## Structure

The integrity of the ship's primary frame, keel, and load-bearing members.

```text
Structure = Superstructure / 2, rounded up
```

**Purpose:** Measure resistance to catastrophic physical failure.

**Effects:**
- Structure is distinct from Hull.
- Critical hits, penetrating attacks, collisions, or severe Hull damage may reduce Structure.
- A ship is destroyed when its Structure is reduced to the destruction threshold defined by the combat rules.

---

## Superstructure

The frame, keel, trusses, mounts, and structural hardware required to secure ship components.

**Purpose:** Support components and determine structural integrity.

**Effects:**
- Each component requires Superstructure equal to its listed Superstructure Cost.
- Every point of Superstructure adds 1 Mass.
- Extra Superstructure may be purchased to reinforce the ship.
- Superstructure contributes to Structure.

---

## Total Engine Rating

The sum of the Ratings of all installed Engines.

```text
Total Engine Rating = Sum of all Engine Ratings
```

**Purpose:** Represent the ship's total installed propulsion capacity.

**Effects:**
- Used to calculate Movement Power Cost.
- Total Engine Rating has no fixed maximum unless another rule imposes one.

---

## Total Mass

The complete Mass of the finished ship.

```text
Total Mass =
Component Mass
+ Superstructure
+ Armor Mass
```

**Purpose:** Determine the ship's physical scale.

**Effects:**
- Determines Mass Factor.
- Determines Size Class.
- Indirectly affects propulsion efficiency and Base Signature.
